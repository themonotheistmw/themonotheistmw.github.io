About The Monotheist

The Monotheist is the personal intellectual and creative space of an independent writer and educator. It brings together work on philosophy, Islamic thought, ethics, education, science, literature and contemporary affairs.

The purpose of this space is to examine ideas carefully, question assumptions, follow arguments wherever the evidence leads, and make serious subjects accessible without stripping them of their complexity.

It encompasses original essays, philosophical reflections, educational resources, book manuscripts and ongoing intellectual projects. Some works are intended for general readers, while others are designed for students and readers seeking a deeper engagement with a subject.

The Monotheist is an independent personal endeavour, unaffiliated with and unsupported by any institution or organisation.
