A computer is an electronic device that accepts data as input, processes it according to a set of instructions (called a program), and produces useful information as output. It can store data for future use and perform tasks very quickly and accurately. The word "computer" originally referred to a person who performed calculations. Over time, machines were built to do this work, and today a computer can do millions of tasks in just one second. Did You Know? The first general-purpose electronic computer, ENIAC, was built in 1945. It weighed about 30 tons and filled an entire room. Today, your smartphone is millions of times more powerful! Key Characteristics of a Computer Characteristic Description Speed Computers perform millions of operations per second. Accuracy They produce correct results if given correct instructions. Storage They can store large amounts of data permanently or temporarily. Automation Once programmed, they work without human intervention.

AI ethics is the field that asks: How should AI be designed, deployed, and governed to ensure it is safe, fair, and beneficial for everyone?
